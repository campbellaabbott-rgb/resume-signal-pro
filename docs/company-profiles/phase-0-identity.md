## What changes

- `employer_segment(token)` is the layoff/LCA matcher's rule (first `~`
  segment; `recruiting~CLIENT` and `recruiting2~CLIENT` for UKG; the whole
  token for `eu~`). `employer_key(source, token)` is `source:segment`. Both are
  IMMUTABLE, inlinable, and executable by `service_role` only. The TypeScript
  mirror is `src/lib/employer-key.ts`; the pglite test holds SQL, TypeScript
  and the matcher's own CASE equal over all 44,519 catalogue boards.
- `get_company_intel`, `get_similar_companies` and `get_company_financials`
  pool by the employer key. The first-segment predicate the slug index serves
  is kept; the segment and the token's own hiring systems are added. A UKG
  client's career sites pool and two clients never do; every `eu~` board is its
  own; a Workday or Oracle tenant pools its sites; a plain slug never pools
  with a same-named tenant on another vendor.
- `get_company_intel` gains `open_by_source` (open roles on this token per
  hiring system). Every other key is unchanged.
- `company_financials.matched_source`: the six slugs whose segment spans two
  vendors are decided by hand (accenture, blackstone, costar and medline on
  workday; lifestance on lever; samsara on greenhouse); 170 are derived from
  the catalogue; trustmark is left unmatched because 20260918200000 read both
  of its sites and rejected them (Trustmark Companies is not Trustmark Corp);
  comcast left the catalogue. A NULL row attaches nowhere.
  Live effect: the Workable board `blackstone` (Blackstone Shooting Sports)
  and the Greenhouse board `costar` (Co-Star) lose the BX and CSGP cards, the
  Workday tenants keep them. The Pinpoint `accenture` board and the Workday
  `lifestance~wd5~Careers` board lose their cards too (one source per row).
- 101000 refuses to replace a live reader whose body is none it knows (the
  20260827140000 body, wave 3's rewrite of it, or its own): it raises with the
  hash it found instead of overwriting a hand edit.
- The 139 tokens two hiring systems carry are read from the catalogue
  (`src/lib/board-collisions.ts`, generated by `npx tsx scripts/board-collisions.ts`).
  Their `/jobs/company/:token` page shows the per-vendor split with each
  board's open roles, and no intel, perks, SEC card, LCA line, claim, Hiring Health
  (with its filings) or similar employers. The browser's employer cache
  answers them with nothing, the layoff lookups skip them, and the bake states
  no places or fields for them.
- The browser's employer cache (`getEmployerCtx`, the compare drawer) keys on
  the employer key: callers pass the posting's source. `src/lib/employer-group.ts`
  is gone.

  the LCA line per token, so a card of a shared token can show a hiring record
  over both boards. A guard holds the LCA line unconditional in the panel;
  fixing these needs the readers to take a source.
- Prerender sibling canonicals: done on `profiles/gate-and-fixes` and kept by
  the integration (`selectLanders` groups by the bake's employer key plus the
  name; a guard holds that key against `employerKey` over the catalogue). See
  `README.md` in this folder.

  `a-board-name-two-employers-share-shows-no-employer-facts.test.tsx` until
  `npx tsx scripts/board-collisions.ts` is run.
