# Company profiles: phases 0 to 2 (branch `profiles/integration`)

Plan: company profiles plan of 2026-10-11 (corrected after its adversarial
review), with the owner's decisions of 2026-10-11 (D1 checkmark list, D2 AI
judges plus an owner spot check, D6 sourced layoff and visa signals, D7 up to
2,000 indexed later, every other decision as the plan recommends).

This integration merges three branches onto `wave3/integration`:
`profiles/identity` and `profiles/gate-and-fixes` (phase 0) and
`profiles/perks` (phases 1 and 2). Wave 3 deploys first; this rides on top.

**The Lovable message is generated by the deploy ledger and is never copied
from this file:** `node scripts/deploy-ledger.mjs --message` at the commit main
holds after the merge (docs/DEPLOY-LEDGER.md). The tables below say what
ships and why, so the generated message can be checked against them.

## What ships

### Phase 0: fix what a profile inherits (live defects first)

| Piece | What is true after it |
|---|---|
| Identity (`profiles/identity`, note: `phase-0-identity.md`) | One employer is its hiring system plus its board: `employer_segment(token)` follows the layoff matcher's rule and `employer_key(source, token)` adds the source (both private to the service role; mirror `src/lib/employer-key.ts`). `get_company_intel`, `get_similar_companies` and `get_company_financials` pool by that key, so Blackstone Inc.'s and CoStar Group's SEC figures no longer reach Blackstone Shooting Sports and Co-Star. `company_financials.matched_source` names the hiring system each SEC row was matched for; a NULL row attaches nowhere. `get_company_intel` also answers `open_by_source`. |
| Shared-token pages | The 139 tokens two hiring systems carry are read from the catalogue (`src/lib/board-collisions.ts`, regenerated by `npx tsx scripts/board-collisions.ts`). Their `/jobs/company/:token` page shows each board's name, hiring system and open roles, and no intel, perks, SEC card, LCA line, claim, Hiring Health (with its filings) or similar employers. The bake asks no intel for them. |
| One hiring-record gate (`profiles/gate-and-fixes`) | `src/lib/hiring-record-gate.ts`, byte-identical with `supabase/functions/_shared/hiring-record-gate.ts`: `canStateFillRate` plus the lap term (no 14-day share and no median on a lap_proven board), the relist control (no 90-day counts at 200 or more taken down with none re-listed) and the 21-day tracking floor (28 on lap_proven boards). | Headcount (D11) | `company_profiles.as_of` exists and is NULL everywhere; every surface prints a headcount only beside its date (`src/lib/headcount.ts`), so none prints today. |
| Prerender | Sibling pages group by the bake's employer key plus the normalised name. A different employer sharing a name still canonicalises to the larger page (the indexable set and the sitemap do not move), but pools none of its openings and is linked as nobody's page. The bake's key reads the token's shape because the facet carries no source; a guard holds it against `employerKey(source, token)` over the whole catalogue: it never joins two boards that key keeps apart. llms.txt's `/companies` line counts the pages the bake made indexable. |

### Phase 1: the perk engine (`profiles/perks`, note: `perks-engine.md`)

The frozen extractor in `supabase/functions/_shared/perks-extract.ts`, the new
`employer-profiles` function (`employer-profiles.2026-10-12.1`, sweep for the
cron's vault key, audit actions for the owner's admin key, `verify_jwt =
false`), eleven private perk tables, the nightly rollup, the leased sweep, the
reader `get_employer_perks(p_token)` and `scripts/perk-audit.mjs`.

### Phase 2: perks on the lander (`profiles/perks`, note: `perks-on-the-lander.md`)

`src/components/jobs/CompanyPerks.tsx` (pure half `src/lib/employer-perks.ts`),
mounted under the intel strip on `/jobs/company/:token`, never on a shared
token. Owner decision D1, a checkmark list built honestly:

- the heading "Perks stated in their job postings" and a line saying a perk
  not listed doesn't mean it isn't offered;
- a check only above the per-company, per-country threshold (n >= 5, k >= 2,
  k/n >= 10%; a stated visa limit needs k >= 3 and never gets a check);
- tap, hover or focus opens "stated in at least k of n open <country>
  postings we could read, read <date>" and the verbatim line, linked to the
  posting while it still serves;
- keyboard and screen-reader accessible (button, `aria-expanded`,
  `aria-controls`, `aria-describedby`, Escape returns focus).

**Every perk ships UNPUBLISHED.** The reader shows a key only while its row in
`employer_profile_perk_publish` is published, its last audit is under 45 days
old (enforced in the reader itself), and the publish pause is off. With no key
live the block renders nothing at all: no heading, no placeholder.

No phase here changes job-board. Phase 3 (vendor benefits capture) takes a
job-board version reserved when it merges, after `.92` (wave 3) and `.93`
(connect-apply).

## After the deploy

1. **Verify.** `bash scripts/verify-deploy.sh`: section 80 (identity and the
   perk engine, two files), section 91 (gate, LCA line, headcount, llms.txt,
   V3, V3b, V7, V7b), section 40 (the census shows nothing unlisted) and
   section 49 (no gap). Record the verified deploy in
   `docs/deploy-ledger.json` `verifiedDeploys`.
2. **Release the hold** (empty the `20261012111000` entry) and send it in a
   second, ledger-generated message. **Run the sweep**: it starts within a
   minute. Watch `node scripts/perk-audit.mjs status`; measure the perk
   tables' size after the top 2,000 boards, and job-board's deep-cursor rate
   over the 2 hours before and after (V6b, within 10%).
3. **The AI-judge audit (D2).** For each candidate key
   (health, dental, vision, life_dis, retire, pto, parental, equity,
   visa_not_offered): `node scripts/perk-audit.mjs draw --perk <key>` writes
   60 publishable rows; independent AI judges grade every row and the owner
   spot-checks at least 10. A row is right only if the quoted line says the
   hiring employer offers it in that posting and the 3-posting spot check
   agrees; one judge's dissent makes a row wrong and the owner's word
   overrides.
4. **Flip the flags.** `node scripts/perk-audit.mjs record --file <file>`: 57
   of 60 publishes the key for 45 days; fewer unpublishes it. Monthly:
   `reaudit-draw`, judge, `record`. A key nobody re-audits stops showing on
   day 45 by itself.
5. **Changelog.** Phase 0's fixes get their entries in all nine locales after
   the verified deploy, each claim read against production. The perks entry
   waits until a key is published and the block is seen live on a lander.

## Open after this integration

- Board cards and the detail panel still read the fill curve, growth and the
  LCA line by token, so a card on a shared token can show a record pooled
  over both boards; `hiringRecordVerdict` (chips, the "Actively hiring"
  filter, the compare drawer) does not yet apply the relist control or the
  tracking floor.
- Account.tsx (watched companies), the GhostJobIndex leaderboard, public-api
  `/v1` and agent-runner still print a lap board's 14-day share.
- `get_employer_perks` decides a shared token from its own perk rows (more
  than one source among them), not from the catalogue. The lander hides the
  block on every catalogue collision; the bake, MCP and `/v1` (phases 4 and 5)
  must apply the catalogue rule too.
- D10 (rename "filled" to "taken down (not relisted)") is not done.
